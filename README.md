# 🌿 AYUSH Skill Mapping Platform

### Smart Academia–Industry Collaboration for Skills, Internships & Placement

> A smart platform connecting **Students, Academia and Industry** through skill mapping, career guidance, courses, internships and placement opportunities.

---

## 🎯 About the Project

The **AYUSH Skill Mapping Platform** is a digital ecosystem designed to bridge the gap between **academic education and industry requirements**.

Students often have difficulty understanding which skills they need for a particular career, what they should learn next, and which internships or job opportunities match their abilities.

Our platform addresses this problem by creating a personalized journey:

**Assess → Map Skills → Identify Gaps → Learn → Apply → Get Hired**

The platform connects three major stakeholders:

```text
        ┌───────────────┐
        │    STUDENTS   │
        └───────┬───────┘
                │
                ▼
       ┌──────────────────┐
       │  SKILL MAPPING   │
       │   & MATCHING     │
       └───────┬──────────┘
               │
        ┌──────┴──────┐
        ▼             ▼
┌─────────────┐ ┌─────────────┐
│   ACADEMIA  │ │   INDUSTRY  │
│             │ │             │
│ Courses     │ │ Internships │
│ Training    │ │ Jobs        │
│ Assessment  │ │ Opportunities│
└─────────────┘ └─────────────┘
```

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎓 Student Portal

* Student profile and skill assessment
* Career preference selection
* Skill-based career recommendations
* Personalized skill-gap analysis
* Recommended courses and learning paths
* Internship opportunities
* Job opportunities
* Personalized career roadmap

### 🧠 Smart Skill Mapping

The platform maps a student's existing skills against the skills required for their desired career.

```text
Student Skills
      ↓
Career Selection
      ↓
Required Skills
      ↓
Skill Gap Analysis
      ↓
Learning Recommendations
      ↓
Internship / Job Matching
```

This helps answer:

> **"What skills do I have, what am I missing, and what should I learn next?"**

### 🏫 Academia Portal

Academic institutions can:

* Publish courses
* Manage student skill profiles
* Track student development
* Identify common skill gaps
* Collaborate with industry
* Share opportunities with students

### 🏢 Industry Portal

Companies can:

* Post internships
* Post job opportunities
* Define required skills
* Discover suitable candidates
* Connect with academic institutions

### 📊 Opportunity Matching

Instead of showing students a generic list of opportunities, the platform focuses on matching opportunities with the student's:

* Skills
* Interests
* Career goals
* Education
* Assessment results

## 💡 Our Differentiator

Traditional job and internship platforms primarily focus on **listing opportunities**.

Our approach starts with the **student's skill profile**.

```text
Traditional Approach

Opportunity → Student applies


Our Approach

Student → Skill Mapping
       → Skill Gap
       → Learning
       → Matching
       → Opportunity
```

The goal is to make **skill mapping and intelligent matching** the foundation of the platform.
